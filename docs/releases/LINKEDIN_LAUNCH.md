## English Post

I am releasing Atlas AI v1.0.0-beta, an open-source AI Financial Intelligence Platform.

Atlas AI starts from a simple product principle: financial calculations should remain deterministic and explainable. AI can help people understand the result, but it should not become the source of financial truth or make the decision for them.

The beta includes a deterministic finance engine, 24-month forecasts, reversible decision scenarios, Financial Memory, reminders, a provider-independent AI Coach, PostgreSQL self-hosting, and a database-free public demo built with fictional data and Mock AI.

The project is designed around "Bring your own AI. Bring your own Database. Deploy anywhere." It supports operator-controlled infrastructure and keeps the public demo free of platform-owned AI keys and persistent financial data.

I built Atlas AI through an AI-assisted product and engineering workflow using Codex for repository analysis, implementation support, testing, and documentation. Product decisions, architecture boundaries, review, and release accountability remained human-led.

## Short Description

Atlas AI is an open-source financial intelligence platform with deterministic calculations, reversible scenarios, optional provider-independent AI explanations, a database-free fictional demo, and PostgreSQL self-hosting.

## Technical Highlights

- Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Prisma, and PostgreSQL
- Deterministic finance engine as the source of truth
- Provider-independent Mock, cloud, local, and OpenAI-compatible adapters
- Database-free per-tab demo with storage-negative tests
- Unit, isolated PostgreSQL integration, and Playwright E2E suites
- Privacy-first execution-mode, credential, and repository boundaries

## Product Management Highlights

- Product constitution and explicit decision principles
- Progressive milestones with deterministic/AI responsibility boundaries
- Clear implemented, experimental, planned, and non-goal status language
- User agency, explainability, privacy, and reversible exploration as release criteria
- Release gates spanning product, architecture, security, QA, and operations
